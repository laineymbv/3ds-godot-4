EditorExportPlatform3DS

The Nintendo 3DS export platform (the editor's Project > Export > Nintendo 3DS).

Inherits: EditorExportPlatform

Exports a project for the 3DS as a .3dsx (run from the Homebrew Launcher) or a .cia (installed with FBI). A .3dsx is written directly: the export template, a title and icon, and a RomFS with the project's .pck. A .cia also needs makerom and bannertool.

Preset options: application/title, application/description, application/author, application/icon (defaults to the project icon), cia/banner, cia/banner_audio. Editor Settings under export/3ds hold the paths to makerom, bannertool and 3dslink, and the console address for "Run on 3DS".

Textures imported as VRAM Compressed are exported as S3TC and decompressed on the 3DS. Lossless textures are stored as WebP, which the 3DS template reads too.
